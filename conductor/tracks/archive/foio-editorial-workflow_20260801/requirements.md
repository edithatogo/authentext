# Requirements: FOI-O Editorial Workflow

## Must

- **FOIO-MUST-001:** Preserve citations, URLs, numerical meaning, epistemic
  qualifiers, legal boundaries, and authorial stance.
- **FOIO-MUST-002:** Provide deterministic verification with a failing result
  for protected-material drift.
- **FOIO-MUST-003:** Produce a portable evidence receipt containing tool,
  skill/profile, version, input/output hashes, checks, and human-gate status.
- **FOIO-MUST-004:** Keep editorial acceptance, submission, and publication as
  explicit human decisions.
- **FOIO-MUST-005:** Integrate through canonical source fragments and generated
  artifacts.

## Should

- **FOIO-SHOULD-001:** Supply a reusable example receipt and workflow checklist.
- **FOIO-SHOULD-002:** Cover success and failure paths in automated tests.
- **FOIO-SHOULD-003:** Link repository evidence to the Conductor and GitHub
  issue hierarchy.

## Could

- **FOIO-COULD-001:** Support additional receipt consumers through the stable
  JSON contract.
- **FOIO-COULD-002:** Add domain-specific protected-token classes in later
  tracks.

## Won't this track

- **FOIO-WONT-001:** Publish, submit, or certify an FOI-O manuscript.
- **FOIO-WONT-002:** Infer factual or legal correctness from token preservation.
- **FOIO-WONT-003:** Depend on host-only metadata or experimental APIs.
