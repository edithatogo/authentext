## Governance boundary

- Retrieval proves availability on the stated date, not future currency.
- `binding` authority requires an identified jurisdiction, venue, contract, or
  publisher rule; none of these general baseline sources is binding by default.
- Source records must preserve title, publisher, URL, retrieval date, scope,
  authority, licence state, freshness state, and supported checks.
- Unknown or unhealthy licence terms fail closed for content reuse. Metadata,
  links, and independently expressed design implications may still be recorded.
- Public guidance may change. Runtime research remains off by default and later
  resolver work must create a new receipt rather than silently refreshing a
  rule.

Detailed adopt, adapt, and reject decisions are recorded in
[`ecosystem-review.md`](./ecosystem-review.md).
